## Scope

`/brand`, mode: Experience. The OSS House brand page in the chosen Signal direction: the refined mark, its motion, colour and type in light and dark, and a sample landing. Not indexed. Its tokens and motion are the site's system.

## Audience and job

The owner first, then anyone building with the brand. Job: see the mark alive, scrub its motion, check colour and type in both themes, see it in use. Constraints: refined logo keeps the original letters, stroke and spacing; logo motion per the owner's brief (dot, ring, snake head; s motion; on scroll the s's leave left into the coil and the snake turns, header keeps only the snake). en + pt.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Motion's night for a software house: the mark is a living field of particles that forms, has depth and parts around the cursor, on a near-black page with one signal yellow. Refuses the dark-green studio page with a 3D object and the centred-logo-over-cards brand book.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black #08090a (light: Motion's off-white #f7f7f4), ink #f2f2ef, signal yellow #ffd60a as the only fill colour, orange only inside the particle gradient. Geist set tight for display, Geist Mono uppercase tracked for labels and values, square hairline buttons, the yellow block CTA with a chevron, hairline-ruled sections, numbered index rows with dotted leaders.

STORY: The visitor sees the mark form out of particles, reads what the page is, scrubs the motion, flips the header state, checks colour and type, sees the landing in use.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Fixed header: logo left (compacts to the snake on scroll), mono section links centre, language and theme right. The 3D particle mark fills the stage (white to yellow to orange across the letters; the head shares the body's density, eye cut out). Bottom-left: "Brand" in Geist 600 and one paragraph. Bottom-right: a mono note, a hairline Replay button and the yellow "See the mark" block.

FORM: Chosen card Signal (Impeccable's pick, safer re-roll round 1 of seed 646b7ddb). Owner's change: the snake head reads like the body, not brighter.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
